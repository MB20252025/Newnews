MYSTERY OF THE 'CARTOGRAPHER' DEEPENS

Professor's disappearance linked to ancient maritime map discovered in Whitby

By Karla Censine · Senior Reporter
Professor Arthur Vale in his study at Whitby Archives, weeks before his disappearance.

Professor Arthur Vale, 58, a renowned historian and cartography expert, has been missing from his Whitby home for over a week. Colleagues reveal he recently uncovered a hidden map in the town's archives that he believed held the key to a centuries-old maritime conspiracy.

"He mentioned a mysterious figure known only as 'The Cartographer'," said Dr. Miriam Shaw, a close colleague. "He became increasingly secretive in the weeks before he vanished. He told me he had found something that would change everything we know about the coastal trade routes."

Vale's study was found ransacked, with rare documents and several 17th-century maps missing. Police are treating the case as a high-priority investigation and conducting extensive searches across Whitby and surrounding coastal areas.

The map he found wasn't just old — it was deliberately hidden. Someone didn't want it found.

Detective Inspector Helen Crawley confirmed that forensic teams are examining the scene for any trace evidence. "We are appealing for anyone who may have seen Professor Vale in the days before his disappearance, or anyone with information about the missing documents, to come forward," she said.
